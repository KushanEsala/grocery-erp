## 1. Purpose

This Agreement records the terms on which the Developer will sell, install and hand over the Grocery ERP system to the Store Owner for use in a grocery retail business. The Store Owner has the opportunity to review the current system before signing. The agreed price covers the current core features and the final reports or minor grocery related additions written on the signing page of this Agreement.

## 2. Current core features included

The current system includes the following core areas:

- POS and cashier workflow: barcode scan and search, product processing, confirmed basket, discounts, tax, cash, card, bank transfer, mobile, split payment, optional customer credit and store credit where enabled, pause and resume sale, shift requirement, receipt print and reprint.
- Product and master data: products, categories, brands, units, tax rates, suppliers, customers, stores, registers, branches, barcodes, purchase and selling prices, company settings, receipt footer, document numbering and role based permissions.
- Purchasing and supplier operations: purchase orders, goods receiving, supplier invoices, supplier balances, supplier payments, purchase returns and optional post-dated cheque lifecycle where enabled.
- Inventory: opening stock, stock levels, batches, expiry dates, FEFO stock issue, stock adjustments, stock transfers, stock counts, reorder alerts, low stock alerts and expiry alerts.
- Sales and customer operations: sales history, sales returns, refunds, customer accounts, customer credit repayments and store credit handling where enabled.
- Accounting and administration: cashier shifts, cash in, cash out, cash drop, expenses, chart of accounts, audit log, users, roles, permissions, backup and restore.
- Reports: dashboard, sales, profit, inventory, expiry, supplier, shift, expense, audit and other reports available in the current system, including export, print or PDF output where available.

## 3. Final scope, reports and extra features

The Store Owner may request final grocery related reports or minor adjustments before signing. Only the items written under "Included final reports and agreed additions" on the signing page are included in the agreed price. Any feature, report, device integration, hardware configuration, accounting change, new module or workflow requested after signing is a change request. A change request may change the cost and delivery time, and work will start only after both parties agree to the revised price and timeline in writing.

## 4. Delivery, acceptance and ownership

The Developer will install the system in the agreed environment and hand over login access after the agreed payment stage is received. The Store Owner will provide the required business details, product data, tax information, receipt details, hardware access and staff availability on time. The Store Owner must test the system within [____] working days after installation. If written issues are not given within that period, the system will be treated as accepted. The Store Owner owns the business data entered into the system. Unless a separate written source code transfer is signed, the Developer retains ownership of the source code, reusable components and development methods, and the Store Owner receives a non-exclusive right to use the deployed system for the agreed store or branches after full payment.

## 5. Support, maintenance and exclusions

For [____] days after final handover, the Developer will correct system faults within the agreed scope without extra service charges. A system fault means a defect in the delivered system that prevents an agreed feature from working as described. Maintenance, training after handover, new reports, new features, data entry work, accounting restructuring, hardware or printer configuration, barcode scanner or scale issues, network issues, operating system issues, MySQL or XAMPP issues, third party changes and changes caused by the Store Owner or another technician are not free defect work unless both parties agree otherwise. Such work will be charged per task or under a separate maintenance agreement.

## 6. Annual server / hosting fee

The system requires an active server / hosting service to remain available online. The Store Owner must pay an annual server / hosting fee of USD 36 per year, or the LKR equivalent calculated on the invoice date. The first year server / hosting fee will be added to the total project bill. From the second year onward, the annual server / hosting fee must be paid before the renewal date. If the fee is not paid within [____] days after a written reminder, the server may be suspended or expire, and the Store Owner may lose access to the system until the outstanding fee is paid and the service is restored. Such suspension or loss of access due to non-payment is not a system fault and is not free maintenance. If the hosting provider, tax, payment gateway charge or currency conversion rate changes after the first year, the Developer may inform the Store Owner in advance and add the actual difference to the relevant annual server bill.
